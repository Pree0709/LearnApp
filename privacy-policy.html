HealthBridge Privacy Policy

Version 1.2 · Effective October 6, 2026

Demonstration only: This sample is for exploring UX-02. It is not a complete or legally reviewed production privacy policy.

What we collect

When you connect Payer A, we receive information the payer makes available through its Patient Access API. This may include claims, coverage, medications and clinical information.

How we use your information

We use imported information to help you review your benefits, care history and costs. We ask for clear permission before using it for another purpose.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell your health information. We share it only when you direct us to, with service providers that help operate HealthBridge, or when legally required.

Information from your device

Connecting a payer does not give us access to your contacts or precise location. If a future feature needs other device information, we will ask separately.

Disconnecting and deletion

You may disconnect Payer A from connection settings at any time. We stop requesting new payer data and delete previously imported payer data within 30 days, except information we must retain by law.

Your choices

You may decide not to connect a payer. You may also withdraw permission later through connection settings.

Questions

A production application should list a real support email, mailing address, privacy contact and any legally required complaint process here.
